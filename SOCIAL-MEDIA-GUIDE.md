## Overview

### Purpose

Maintain a **consistent online presence** for X4O (Pty) Limited across social media platforms to:
- Increase brand awareness
- Generate leads for consulting and coaching services
- Establish thought leadership in enterprise advisory
- Engage with potential clients and partners

### Target Frequency

| Platform | Recommended Frequency | Best Posting Times (SAST) |
|----------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| **Facebook** | 3-5 posts/week | 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM |
| **LinkedIn** | 3-5 posts/week | 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM |
| **Instagram** | 3-5 posts/week | 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM |
| **YouTube** | 1-2 videos/month | 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM |

### Content Mix (60-30-10 Rule)

- **60% Educational** - Industry insights, tips, how-to guides
- **30% Engagement** - Questions, polls, user-generated content
- **10% Promotional** - Service highlights, booking CTAs, special offers

## Meta Business Suite Setup (Facebook/Instagram)

### Initial Setup

#### Step 1: Access Meta Business Suite

1. Go to **https://business.facebook.com**
2. Log in with the **Facebook account** that manages the X4O Facebook Page
3. Select **X4O Consultants** business page

#### Step 2: Connect Instagram (Optional)

1. Click **Settings** (gear icon) in top-right
2. Go to **Instagram accounts**
3. Click **Connect account**
4. Log in to X4O Instagram account
5. Authorize the connection

### Creating and Scheduling Posts

#### Step 1: Navigate to Planner

1. Click **Planner** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see a calendar view of your scheduled posts

#### Step 2: Create a New Post

1. Click **Create post** button
2. Choose post type:
   - **Facebook post** - Regular Facebook update
   - **Instagram feed post** - Instagram grid post
   - **Instagram Story** - 24-hour story
   - **Instagram Reel** - Short-form video
   - **Cross-post** - Publish to both Facebook and Instagram

#### Step 3: Compose Your Post

1. **Write caption:**
   - Keep it concise (under 250 characters for best engagement)
   - Include relevant hashtags (3-5 tags)
   - Add call-to-action (CTA)

2. **Add media:**
   - Click **Add photo/video**
   - Upload image (recommended: 1200x630px for Facebook, 1080x1080px for Instagram)
   - Or select from previously uploaded media

3. **Tag location (optional):**
   - Click **Check in**
   - Search for "Durbanville, Cape Town" or "Cape Town, South Africa"

#### Step 4: Schedule the Post

1. Click **Schedule** button (instead of "Publish")
2. Select date and time
   - **Best times:** 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM SAST (weekdays)
3. Click **Schedule post**

#### Step 5: Verify Scheduled Posts

1. Go to **Planner** view
2. See all scheduled posts on calendar
3. Click any post to edit or reschedule
4. Click **⋮ (three dots)** → **Delete** to remove

## Buffer Setup (Multi-Platform)

### Initial Setup

#### Step 1: Create Buffer Account

1. Go to **https://buffer.com**
2. Click **Get started now** (Free plan)
3. Sign up with email or Google account

#### Step 2: Connect Social Accounts

1. Click **Channels** in left sidebar
2. Click **Connect a channel**
3. Select platform:
   - Facebook Page (choose X4O Consultants page)
   - LinkedIn (personal or company page)
   - Instagram (via Facebook connection)
4. Authorize Buffer to access each account

#### Step 3: Set Posting Schedule

1. Go to **Settings** → **Posting Schedule**
2. For each channel, set preferred posting times:

**Example schedule:**
- **Monday:** 10:00 AM (LinkedIn), 11:30 AM (Facebook)
- **Tuesday:** 9:00 AM (Instagram), 12:00 PM (LinkedIn)
- **Wednesday:** 10:00 AM (Facebook), 11:00 AM (LinkedIn)
- **Thursday:** 9:30 AM (Instagram), 12:00 PM (Facebook)
- **Friday:** 10:00 AM (LinkedIn), 11:00 AM (Facebook)

### Creating and Scheduling Posts with Buffer

#### Method 1: Queue Posts

1. Click **Queue** in left sidebar
2. Click **Create post**
3. Write caption and upload media
4. Click **Add to Queue**
5. Buffer automatically schedules based on your preset times

#### Method 2: Custom Schedule

1. Click **Create post**
2. Write caption and upload media
3. Click **Schedule post**
4. Choose specific date and time
5. Click **Schedule**

#### Method 3: Browser Extension

1. Install **Buffer browser extension** for Chrome/Firefox
2. When reading an article or viewing content, click Buffer icon
3. Add comment and schedule directly from any webpage

## Content Best Practices

### Image Guidelines

**Dimensions:**
- **Facebook:** 1200 x 630 px (landscape)
- **Instagram Feed:** 1080 x 1080 px (square) or 1080 x 1350 px (portrait)
- **Instagram Story:** 1080 x 1920 px (9:16 ratio)
- **LinkedIn:** 1200 x 627 px (landscape)

**File Format:**
- JPG for photos
- PNG for graphics with transparency
- MP4 for videos (H.264 codec)

**File Size:**
- Max 15 MB for images
- Max 200 MB for videos (Facebook/Instagram)

**Quality:**
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 1000px width)
- Avoid blurry or pixelated images
- Ensure good lighting and contrast

### Caption Best Practices

**Length:**
- **Facebook:** 40-80 words (under 250 characters for best engagement)
- **Instagram:** 125-150 characters for main caption, details in first comment
- **LinkedIn:** 150-300 words for articles, 50-100 for regular posts

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (first sentence) - Grab attention
2. **Value** (middle) - Provide insight, information, or benefit
3. **Call-to-Action** (end) - What should they do next?

**Formatting:**
- Use emojis strategically (1-3 per post)
- Break text into short paragraphs (1-2 sentences each)
- Use bullet points (✅ ✓ •) for lists
- Include line breaks for readability

### Hashtag Strategy

**General Guidelines:**
- Use **3-5 hashtags** per post (Facebook/LinkedIn)
- Use **5-10 hashtags** per post (Instagram)
- Mix popular and niche hashtags
- Create a branded hashtag (#X4O, #X4OConsultants)
- Place hashtags at the end of caption or in first comment (Instagram)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Overusing hashtags (looks spammy)
- ❌ Irrelevant hashtags (decreases engagement)
- ❌ Generic hashtags like #love #instagood (too broad)

## Engagement Guidelines

### Responding to Comments

**Response Time:**
- Aim to respond within **24 hours**
- Prioritize negative comments (respond within 2-4 hours)
- Acknowledge positive comments with likes or brief replies

**Response Templates:**

**Positive Comment:**
```
Thanks for your comment, [Name]! We're glad you found this helpful.
Feel free to reach out if you have any questions: info@x4o.co.za
```

**Question:**
```
Great question, [Name]! [Brief answer]. For more details, visit
https://x4o.co.za or email info@x4o.co.za.
```

**Negative Feedback:**
```
Hi [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. We'd love to discuss
this further. Please email us at info@x4o.co.za or call [phone] so we
can address your concerns directly.
```

### Community Management

**Daily Tasks:**
- Check notifications for comments/messages (morning and afternoon)
- Respond to direct messages within 24 hours
- Like and reply to comments on your posts
- Moderate spam or inappropriate comments (hide/delete)

**Weekly Tasks:**
- Engage with partner pages (like, comment, share their content)
- Join relevant groups (LinkedIn groups, Facebook groups for SMEs)
- Share industry news or articles from thought leaders

**Monthly Tasks:**
- Review analytics to see top-performing posts
- Adjust content strategy based on engagement data
- Update profile information, cover photos if needed

## Performance Tracking

### Key Metrics to Monitor

| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **Reach** | Number of unique users who saw your post | 500-1000/post |
| **Engagement Rate** | Likes + Comments + Shares ÷ Reach | 2-5% |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | Clicks on links ÷ Reach | 1-2% |
| **Follower Growth** | New followers per month | 20-50/month |
| **Website Traffic** | Visitors from social media | 50-100/month |

### Where to Find Analytics

**Facebook:**
- Meta Business Suite → Insights → Overview
- View post performance, audience demographics, engagement trends

**Instagram:**
- Meta Business Suite → Insights → Content
- View reach, impressions, profile visits, website clicks

**LinkedIn:**
- LinkedIn Page → Analytics → Updates
- View impressions, engagement rate, clicks, followers

**Buffer:**
- Analytics tab → Overview
- Compare performance across all platforms
